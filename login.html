Kyambogo University (KYU) is one of Uganda’s largest public universities, widely recognized for its focus on science, technology, and teacher education. Located on Kyambogo Hill (also known as Banda Hill), the main campus sits approximately 8 kilometers east of Kampala’s central business district along the Kampala–Jinja highway.
